    Leave and keep your bag?

    The round ends and the bag goes to your balance.

    How to play

    • Tap tiles to find Diamonds. A spider ends the round.
    • Every safe tile grows your bag. Leave any time to keep it.
    • Sonar (40 Credits) marks one safe tile. The button beside a row (15) counts its spiders.
    • Cash out at 1.5× or more to unlock the next level.
    • Diamonds and Credits are virtual and have no cash value.
    • Keys: arrows move, Enter picks, L leaves, S uses Sonar.

    Settings

    Haptics are not supported on this device.

    Reset progress?

    Diamonds, Credits and unlocked levels go back to the start.
